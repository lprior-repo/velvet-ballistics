## Audit finding
**The `index_status` and `index_workflow` Fjall keyspaces are NOT populated by `submit_artifact`.**

Evidence:
1. `put_status_index` defined at `crates/vb_storage/src/batch.rs:280` on `Batch`.
2. `put_workflow_index` defined at `crates/vb_storage/src/batch.rs:293` on `Batch`.
3. `submit_artifact` at `crates/vb_storage/src/admission.rs:230-236` (and its internal helpers) has ZERO references to either method (verified via `rg`).
4. Keyspaces defined at `crates/vb_storage/src/journal/core.rs:59-60` as `fjall::Keyspace`.
5. Keyspaces are only populated in test code: `batch/tests.rs:106, 118, 405, 408, 516, 518, 840, 843, 954, 956, 2084, 2087`, `journal/tests.rs:934, 937, 940, 962-964, 1050, 1053`, `index_tests.rs:27`.
6. Recovery (`recover_runtime_frame_seed_from_events`) reads events, not index keyspaces.
7. Master doc `velvet-ballistics-MASTER.md` does not mention these keyspaces.
8. No `pending_actions` API or gate exists (only `UnsupportedRecoveryState::pending_actions: bool` flag, which is unrelated).
9. No `master §44.15` exists; §44 has 24 numbered points (no "operational affordances" framing).

## Regression-guard test
- Added `submit_artifact_does_not_populate_index_status_or_workflow_keyspaces` at end of `crates/vb_storage/src/admission/tests.rs`.
- Asserts: after `submit_artifact`, both `index_status` and `index_workflow` keyspaces are empty.
- Purpose: if a future change wires the indexes, this test fails and forces an explicit update of the audit record and evidence matrix.

## Wiring is out of scope
- Wiring requires plumbing `RunId` and `WorkflowId` through `submit_artifact` (currently it only has `&CompiledWorkflow`).
- The `CompiledWorkflow` does carry a `WorkflowId` (verified at `vb_core::CompiledWorkflow`), but `RunId` is a per-admission concept that `submit_artifact` does not currently take.
- Wiring is a follow-up bead, not part of this audit.
