## Performance-Layer Decision

- Hot path: `aggregate_resource_budget` over 1000-element `&[RunMetrics]`.
- Allocation: zero post-init allocations; the bench input is built once.
- Dispatch: static, monomorphised, no trait objects.
- Branch shape: single branchless `for` loop; all conditionals on `Option` are
  compile-time eliminated for the bench workload.
- Benchmark evidence: see Criterion output above (~378.84 ns / 1000 runs).
- Second-ring evidence: not required — this bead is a cleanup + new helper,
  not a claim of zero-cost removal of a previously-existing abstraction.
