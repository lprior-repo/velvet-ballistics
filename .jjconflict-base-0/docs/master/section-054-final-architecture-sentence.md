## 54. Final Architecture Sentence

`velvet-ballistics` is a local-first, single-server, compiler-enforced durable orchestration system for AI-authored workflows.

Its Rust SDK is the authoring language. Its compiler is the judge. Its accepted artifact is the deployable unit. Its runtime is a history-first durable orchestrator. Its reports are the feedback loop that lets humans and AI adapt software without turning production into a swamp.
