# Pre-persisted admission frame ownership

On the supported current-artifact SubmitPrePersisted route, a refused admission append returns its original error and releases the acquired frame and admission quota. Capacity-one resources remain reusable. Existing durable prefix and authority remain unchanged for a before-effect refusal. Current budget, digest, pinned policy and positive issuance provenance must be used in the regression fixture. Batch semantics and error priorities remain unchanged.
